Marketing Autopilot

Marketing Autopilot is a private marketing workflow operated for Table Rock Press. It prepares approved Table Rock Press content and publishes it to authorized social accounts only after the account owner grants the required platform permissions.

What the app does

  • Organizes approved Table Rock Press marketing copy and creative assets.
  • Creates platform-appropriate media variants while preserving the original files.
  • Schedules and publishes approved content to connected social accounts.
  • Tracks publishing results and surfaces failures that need attention.

Platform access

Social account access is used only for the account owner's authorized publishing workflow. Marketing Autopilot does not sell social account data, does not publish to accounts that have not been explicitly connected, and does not enable paid services without owner approval.

Data and privacy

Connector credentials are stored server-side and are not exposed to the browser. Source marketing assets remain preserved, and derived media variants can be regenerated. See the Privacy Policy.

Operated for Table Rock Press. This page exists so platform reviewers can verify the application's purpose and public availability without signing in.
