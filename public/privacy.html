Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026

Marketing Autopilot is a private marketing workflow operated for Table Rock Press. It is used to manage approved marketing content and publish it to social accounts that the account owner explicitly authorizes.

Information processed

The service may process social-platform account identifiers, authorization tokens, publishing metadata, post identifiers, approved marketing copy, creative assets, campaign metrics, and operational logs needed to run and troubleshoot the service.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare and publish approved content, track publishing outcomes, maintain the service, and alert the owner to failures or sales events. Marketing Autopilot does not sell personal information or social account data.

Credential handling

Platform credentials are stored server-side and are not returned to the browser after storage. Access is limited to the functions required to operate connected publishing workflows.

Data retention and deletion

Operational data is retained only as needed for the publishing workflow, audit history, and troubleshooting. Connected platform access can be revoked at the platform, and stored connector credentials can be removed from Marketing Autopilot.

Third-party platforms

When an account is connected, the relevant platform's own terms and privacy policy also apply. Marketing Autopilot requests only permissions needed for the configured publishing workflow.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the Table Rock Press contact information associated with the connected platform account.
